# Decision — Public Guide Hybrid V1

Date: 2026-08-20

Status: **OWNER-DIRECTED WORKING DECISION — NOT YET MERGED TO CANONICAL `product-recovery`**

Branch: `docs/public-guide-hybrid-v1`

## Decision

The public pre-purchase printed artifact moves from a pure 16-page expert guide to a hybrid:

1. **8-page A5 booklet** — changes how the reader thinks about function.
2. **Separate observation card „Moje trzy rzeczy”** — makes the idea useful without becoming a tracker, score or DIY programme.

## Central idea

Primary frame:

> **Sprawność to nie tylko to, co jeszcze potrafisz zrobić. Liczy się również, ile swobody i zapasu zostaje Ci przy robieniu rzeczy, które są dla Ciebie ważne.**

Supporting observation:

> **Jednym z pierwszych sygnałów zmiany może być to, że ta sama rzecz zaczyna kosztować Cię więcej, zanim przestaniesz ją umieć.**

The second sentence is a hook / observation, not the total definition of Studio Las or its target audience. This preserves fit for healthy, capable clients who want to maintain or increase reserve before any decline appears.

## Why the prior V3 is not the final public artifact

V3 remains strong source copy but repeats several principles and carries too much Studio Las explanation relative to independent reader utility. The risk is a long branded brochure rather than a physical object worth keeping.

The new hybrid intentionally separates:

- **booklet = information + identity + reframing**;
- **card = utility + conversation + keep value**.

## What is preserved from V3

- life before exercises;
- reserve as a metaphor, not a score;
- same functional complaint can have different meanings;
- question → signal → meaning → decision;
- trainer/medical boundary;
- long-term autonomy;
- calm, non-fitness language.

## What is reduced or moved out

- detailed description of Studio Las method;
- detailed PWD description;
- long fit/not-fit sales section;
- repeated explanation of measurement philosophy;
- repeated explanation of process adaptation.

These remain available in product/source documentation and can be used on the landing page or later customer material rather than inside the public booklet.

## Explicitly rejected

Do not turn the observation card into:

- a scored test;
- green/red status;
- universal functional battery;
- daily/weekly tracker;
- 12-week DIY programme;
- wellness planner;
- habit checklist;
- medical screening tool;
- gamification.

1. targeted evidence/safety review of the final copy;
