# 00 Product Model

## Purpose

This document defines what the Studio Las product is.

It protects the project from confusing the support tool with the real product.

The product is not Studio Las OS.

The product is the Studio Las Method: a private trainer-led method for guiding a person from pain, fear, uncertainty, or physical decline toward functional capacity, trust in the body, and long-term independence.

## Core statement

A Studio Las client does not buy workouts.

A Studio Las client does not buy an app.

A Studio Las client does not buy access to data, dashboards, streaks, or plans.

A Studio Las client buys guided interpretation, safe progression, and a structured process led by a trainer.

The product is the relationship between:

- the person,
- the trainer,
- the method,
- the environment,
- the paper guidance,
- the observed signals,
- the final report and next decision.

Studio Las OS exists only to support this relationship.

## Product definition

Studio Las is a private method of human guidance.

Its job is to help the client:

- understand what is currently limiting them,
- reduce fear around movement,
- rebuild basic physical capacity,
- notice signals from the body without becoming obsessed with them,
- regain confidence in daily movement,
- become more independent over time.

The product is delivered through a trainer-led process, not through software.

Software may support the process, but it must never become the center of the process.

## What the client really buys

The client buys:

1. Interpretation  
   The trainer helps the client understand what the body is communicating.

2. Direction  
   The trainer chooses what matters now and what can wait.

3. Safety  
   The trainer adjusts intensity, range, complexity, and expectations to the client’s current state.

4. Progression  
   The trainer decides when to repeat, regress, pause, or move forward.

5. Continuity  
   The process connects diagnosis, sessions, home guidance, observations, and reports.

6. Independence  
   The client should need less reassurance over time, not more app dependency.

## What is not the product

The product is not:

- Studio Las OS,
- a fitness application,
- a wellness application,
- a habit tracker,
- a quantified-self dashboard,
- a training plan library,
- an AI coach,
- a SaaS subscription,
- a wearable data platform,
- a gamified motivation system,
- a collection of PDFs,
- a report generator.

These may be tools, records, or outputs.

They are not the product.

## Product doctrine

The trainer is the product carrier.

The method is the product logic.

The environment supports the client’s state.

Paper carries the morning.

The app records only the signal.

The report reveals the pattern and supports the next decision.

Core rule:

> Paper guides the morning.  
> Trainer gives meaning.  
> App records the signal.  
> Report shows the pattern.

## Decision rule

A Studio Las product decision is good only if it strengthens the trainer-led method.

Before approving any product or OS change, ask:

1. Does this make the trainer’s decision better?
2. Does this make the client calmer, clearer, or more independent?
3. Does this reduce chaos rather than add another task?
4. Does this work without the client starting the morning from the phone?
5. Does this support the future report and next decision?
6. Does this avoid gamification, comparison, pressure, and unnecessary tracking?
7. Does this protect the trainer’s role instead of replacing it?

If the answer is no, the change does not belong in Studio Las.

## Role of Studio Las OS

Studio Las OS is an internal support system.

Its role is to:

- preserve client process history,
- record short signals,
- support trainer memory,
- organize observations,
- help prepare reports,
- make patterns easier to see.

Its role is not to:

- motivate the client through screen mechanics,
- replace trainer judgment,
- become the primary client experience,
- become a separate product,
- increase screen time,
- collect data because it is technically possible.

The app must remain quiet, narrow, and subordinate to the method.

## Product boundary

Studio Las can use technology.

Studio Las cannot become technology-led.

Studio Las can measure.

Studio Las cannot become measurement-led.

Studio Las can use reports.

Studio Las cannot reduce the person to a report.

Studio Las can use AI later to support trainer analysis.

Studio Las cannot give the client an AI replacement for the trainer.

## Long-term product test

In five years, Studio Las should still be understandable without opening the app.

A new trainer should be able to read the product documents and understand:

- what the client is actually buying,
- how the trainer should think,
- why fewer measurements can be better than more,
- why paper matters,
- why reports are decisions,
- why independence is more important than engagement.

If the product can only be explained through screens, flows, or features, the product model has failed.

## Implication for future work

All future Studio Las OS work must start from this model.

Do not design features first.

Define the trainer decision first.

Then define the smallest signal needed.

Then decide whether the signal belongs on paper, in conversation, in the app, or in the report.

The app is always the last layer, never the first.
