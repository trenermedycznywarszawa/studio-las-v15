# 02 Non-Negotiables

## Purpose

This document defines the non-negotiable constraints for Studio Las and Studio Las OS.

These constraints cannot be overridden by Product, Architecture, PRD, implementation, code, website copy, forms, migrations, or runtime convenience.

## Primary non-negotiable

> Paper guides the morning.  
> Trainer gives meaning.  
> App records the signal.  
> Report shows the pattern.

This is the central constitutional constraint.

Any change that breaks this order is rejected.

## Non-negotiable 1 — Product is the method

The product is the trainer-led Studio Las Method.

The product is not the app.

The product is not the dashboard.

The product is not the report file.

The product is not a database.

The product is not a SaaS account.

## Non-negotiable 2 — Trainer owns meaning

The trainer owns:

- interpretation,
- progression,
- regression,
- safety judgment,
- report meaning,
- client-safe publication,
- next-step decisions.

Automation may support organization.

Automation must not own meaning.

## Non-negotiable 3 — Paper-first morning

The client must be able to begin the morning without the app.

The paper guide carries the morning ritual.

The app may record a short signal later.

The app must not become the morning guide.

## Non-negotiable 4 — App as record, not ritual

The app records selected signals.

The app must not become:

- a habit tracker,
- a streak system,
- a motivation engine,
- a feed,
- a daily dashboard,
- a compliance scoreboard,
- a source of automatic meaning.

## Non-negotiable 5 — Report as pattern, not dashboard

The report shows patterns across the process.

The report must not become a raw metric export.

The report must not shame the client.

The report must not replace trainer interpretation.

The report must support the next decision.

## Non-negotiable 6 — Client-safe by design

The client may see only intentional client-safe surfaces.

The client must not see by default:

- private trainer notes,
- raw hypotheses,
- internal risk reasoning,
- draft reports,
- technical identifiers,
- other client data,
- raw AI output,
- hidden data quality notes.

Client-facing means intentionally written, calm, useful, and safe.

## Non-negotiable 7 — Minimal purposeful data

Data may be collected only when it supports:

- trainer decisions,
- process continuity,
- safety,
- client-safe guidance,
- report patterns.

Data must not be collected because it is interesting, technically easy, or potentially useful someday.

## Non-negotiable 8 — No product drift

Studio Las OS must not become:

- a fitness app,
- a wellness app,
- a habit tracker,
- a quantified-self dashboard,
- a SaaS product,
- a client-facing AI coach,
- a wearable platform,
- a gamified engagement system,
- a product that increases screen time.

## Non-negotiable 9 — Privacy and dignity

Health-related and personal data must be treated as sensitive.

Privacy claims must match actual data flow.

Forms, processors, storage, reports, and client surfaces must protect client dignity.

No convenience decision may override privacy or client-safe boundaries.

## Non-negotiable 10 — Lower layers cannot create truth

Website pages, blog posts, code, migrations, test data, demo data, local notes, and runtime behavior may not create product truth.

They may only project or implement approved truth.

## Rejection rule

Reject any change that:

- makes the client start with the phone,
- makes the app the coach,
- makes data more important than trainer meaning,
- makes the report a dashboard,
- exposes trainer-only information,
- creates duplicate truth,
- increases self-monitoring pressure,
- turns Studio Las into a generic app/product.

## Final rule

A feature can be technically useful and still constitutionally wrong.

Constitution decides what Studio Las is allowed to become.
