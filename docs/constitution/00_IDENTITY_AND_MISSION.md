# 00 Identity and Mission

## Purpose

This document defines the constitutional identity of Studio Las and Studio Las OS.

It is the highest identity layer below the future Mission layer.

It does not define features.

It does not define UI.

It does not define database schema.

It defines what the project is allowed to become.

## Studio Las identity

Studio Las is not a gym.

Studio Las is not a fitness app.

Studio Las is not a wellness product.

Studio Las is a private trainer-led method for helping a person regain functional capacity, trust in their body, and long-term independence.

The center of Studio Las is the guided human process.

The trainer-led relationship is the product.

## Studio Las OS identity

Studio Las OS is not the product.

Studio Las OS is an internal support system for the Studio Las Method.

It exists to preserve continuity, record selected signals, support trainer decisions, and help reports show patterns.

Studio Las OS must remain subordinate to the method.

The OS must never become the main thing the client buys, sees, or depends on.

## Core constitutional rule

> Paper guides the morning.  
> Trainer gives meaning.  
> App records the signal.  
> Report shows the pattern.

This rule has priority over every feature, screen, data model, migration, report, workflow, and implementation decision.

## Product truth

The product is the Studio Las Method.

The method includes:

- trainer-led interpretation,
- individual 1:1 guidance,
- paper-first home guidance,
- minimal app-recorded signals,
- reports that show patterns,
- long-term client independence.

The product is not:

- the app,
- the dashboard,
- the database,
- the report template,
- the website,
- the pricing page,
- the intake form,
- the implementation.

## Mission protection

Studio Las should help a person become more capable, less afraid of movement, more connected to their body, and more independent over time.

This must be done without turning the person into a set of metrics, scores, streaks, dashboards, or automated recommendations.

## Human authority

The trainer owns meaning.

The trainer owns interpretation.

The trainer owns progression and regression decisions.

The trainer owns the final client-safe report meaning.

Technology may support the trainer.

Technology may not replace the trainer.

## Client dignity

The client is a person in a guided relationship, not a SaaS user first.

The client must not be pressured into excessive self-monitoring.

The client must not be shamed by signals, metrics, completion status, or report language.

The client must not be asked to start the morning with the app.

## Long-term direction

Studio Las may grow in documentation, systems, reports, and internal tools.

It must not grow into:

- a fitness app,
- a habit tracker,
- a wellness dashboard,
- a quantified-self product,
- a SaaS platform,
- a client-facing AI coach,
- a gamified engagement loop,
- a screen-time product.

## Final rule

If a future change makes Studio Las OS more central than the trainer-led method, the change violates the Constitution.
