# 03 Product Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines the constitutional boundaries of Studio Las and Studio Las OS.

It protects the project from becoming a different product through small reasonable-sounding changes.

## Primary boundary

Studio Las is a private trainer-led method.

Studio Las OS is an internal support system.

The OS may support the method.

The OS may not become the method.

## What Studio Las is

Studio Las is:

- private,
- trainer-led,
- 1:1,
- calm,
- method-based,
- focused on functional capacity,
- focused on trust in the body,
- focused on long-term independence,
- supported by paper, app signals, and reports.

Studio Las is not a generic training service.

Studio Las is not a gym membership.

Studio Las is not a content platform.

Studio Las is not an app subscription.

## What Studio Las OS is allowed to do

Studio Las OS may:

- preserve client process history,
- record selected trainer decisions,
- record minimal client signals,
- support trainer review,
- support report preparation,
- protect client-safe boundaries,
- organize paper-first guidance assignments,
- help the trainer notice patterns.

## What Studio Las OS must not do

Studio Las OS must not:

- replace trainer judgment,
- become the client's morning interface,
- coach the client automatically,
- diagnose,
- auto-progress or auto-regress a client,
- publish report meaning without trainer approval,
- create streaks, badges, rankings, or compliance pressure,
- become a wellness dashboard,
- become a quantified-self tool,
- become a public SaaS product,
- become a product sold separately from the 1:1 relationship.

## Client access boundary

Client access exists because of an active Studio Las relationship.

The app is not sold separately.

The client should lose access when the guided relationship ends, unless a future explicit governance decision says otherwise.

Client access must never imply that the app itself is the product.

## Website boundary

The website may communicate Studio Las.

The website may not redefine Studio Las.

The website may publish approved truth about:

- offer,
- diagnostic visit,
- method,
- process,
- trainer,
- place,
- contact path,
- client expectations.

The website may not independently define:

- product identity,
- price,
- package structure,
- medical claims,
- privacy promises,
- report meaning,
- app role.

## Blog and education boundary

Educational content may support trust and understanding.

Educational content may not become doctrine.

Blog posts must not redefine the method, create medical promises, or introduce claims that are not governed elsewhere.

## Forms boundary

Forms may collect information only for approved intake, contact, or process purposes.

Forms may not define privacy practice independently.

Forms may not collect more data than the method needs.

Forms must not create medical, diagnostic, or legal promises beyond approved governance.

## Data boundary

Data exists to support trainer decisions, process continuity, client-safe guidance, and reports.

Data does not exist for dashboards, curiosity, future speculation, marketing profiling, or quantified-self analytics.

## AI and automation boundary

AI may later support trainer-facing organization, summarization, analysis, or drafting.

AI must not:

- coach the client,
- decide meaning,
- diagnose,
- publish report content,
- decide progression,
- replace the trainer,
- create automated client-facing recommendations.

## Growth boundary

Studio Las may grow in quality.

It may grow in structure.

It may grow in documentation.

It may grow in internal tools.

It must not grow by adding features that make the method less human, less trainer-led, less paper-first, or more screen-dependent.

## Final rule

If a boundary is unclear, choose the smaller, quieter, more trainer-led option until Product and Architecture explicitly approve otherwise.
