# Studio Las — Research Evidence

## Purpose

This directory stores external research, benchmark synthesis, and evidence notes that may inform future Studio Las decisions.

Research is deliberately separated from Product, Architecture, PRD, implementation, code, and runtime truth.

## Authority rule

Nothing in `docs/research/` may silently redefine accepted Studio Las truth.

Research may support, challenge, or qualify a future decision. A canonical change must still happen at the correct owner layer and pass the required governance/audit gate.

In particular, research may not by itself change:

- Constitution,
- Product doctrine,
- accepted Architecture Contracts,
- PRD scope,
- trainer decision ownership,
- client-safe publication boundaries,
- privacy/legal requirements,
- pricing,
- runtime behavior.

## Evidence labels

Research documents should state one of:

- `EXPLORATORY` — discovery only;
- `PARTIAL EVIDENCE` — useful but incomplete;
- `QUALIFIED STRATEGIC INPUT` — sufficiently coherent to inform later product reasoning, but not canonical truth;
- `AUDITED INPUT` — independently audited and safe to use within stated qualifications.

A research document must not call itself `AUDITED INPUT` unless a separate audit explicitly reaches that verdict.
