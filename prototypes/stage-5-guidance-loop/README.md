This isolated, deterministic, offline, session-memory prototype validates the highest-risk behavior of the twelve-week guidance and adaptation loop. It is a decision instrument, not an early production application.

## Decision this prototype must support

Determine whether Studio Las can preserve one calm current instruction across paper, app, and deliberate hybrid while:

- Damian remains the only source of meaning and adaptation;
- old actionable guidance cannot compete with the current release;
- the smallest decision-relevant response does not become compliance tracking;
- ordinary completion and silence do not create administrative work;
- the complete loop remains possible manually without a portal.

## Five primary risks

1. the client cannot identify the one current authoritative instruction;
2. paper and app become separately maintained truth;
3. revision leaves old instructions actionable;
4. Damian becomes an administrator rather than the trainer and decision owner;
5. technology becomes required for a process that must work manually.

Visual polish is deliberately subordinate to these risks. Schema, provider, storage, runtime, AI, and production behavior remain outside the experiment.

## Internal manual preflight

### Standard app path

1. Open `app-primary` and start the manual path.
2. Confirm one current release and no trainer-only rationale in the client surface.
3. Save `done_as_planned`; confirm the trainer queue remains empty.
4. Save `stopped` or a question; confirm it enters review without judgment.
5. Try to decide before explicit event review; confirm fail-closed.

## Moderated validation procedure — not yet completed

### Purpose

Test understanding, decision safety, and error handling; do not try to prove product success. This is a moderated test of a fictional, offline Stage 5 prototype, not a medical service, diagnosis, or test using client data.

### Participants and organisation

- Include 5–7 people close to Studio Las's intended users. Use no real client data and do not recruit or contact anyone on the owner's behalf.
- Reserve 30–40 minutes per person.
- Before each task, the moderator does not explain the interface. Ask the participant to say aloud what they understand and expect.
- State that the prototype is fictional and offline, and that the participant may stop at any time without giving a reason.

### Fixed tasks

1. Identify the current guidance and its purpose.
2. Explain what to do at its stop or reduction boundary.
3. Distinguish an execution response from a question or important context.
4. Complete the `manual-no-portal` path.
5. Respond to an outdated or risky paper guidance situation.
6. State when to contact a human rather than acting alone.

### Short questions after the tasks

Ask no more than these six questions:

1. What do you understand to be the one current instruction?
2. What would make you shorten, stop, or seek help outside Studio Las?
3. Which part, if any, felt uncertain?
4. What did you expect to happen after recording a response or question?
5. Did anything imply judgment, pressure, or a score?
6. At which point would you contact a person instead of continuing alone?
